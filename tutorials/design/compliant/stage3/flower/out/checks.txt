=== scale law at constant pressure (why the module is fractal) ===
wind moment ~ q Cd (pi/4) D^2 x height(~0.9 D) ~ D^3; wrinkling moment (pi/2) p r^3 with r ~ D ~ D^3: the safety factor is scale-free.
hoop tension p r ~ D: fabric thickness grows with scale (or pressure falls as 1/D, then SF ~ 1/D: large flowers lose).
free-hose pointing error F h^2 / (2 E t pi r^3) ~ D^4 / D^3 ~ D: small flowers point better; cable stays make it ~D^3/D^2 ~ D too, but 100x smaller.
head mass ~ D^2 (skins) while a rigid dish ~ D^2.5-3: the skin head is 17 kg at 4.2 m against 140 kg today.
eversion force p pi r^2 ~ D^2 ~ head weight: setup by inflation works at every scale.
