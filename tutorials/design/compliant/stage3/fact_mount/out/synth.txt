STEP 1  desired motions: rotation about the vertical through F (azimuth, 220 deg), rotation about the horizontal through F (elevation, 71 deg, el 12-83),
        and fine tip/tilt/focus of the dish about its vertex (+-20 mrad, +-30 mm). Rotation about F only, F fixed on the tube: Hashemi figs 3-5.
STEP 2  freedom spaces: azimuth and elevation are each 1 DOF Type 1 (a rotation line, thesis 3.2.1); at an instant the pair is 2 DOF Type 1 (a disk of
        intersecting rotation lines through F, 3.3.1). The fine motion is 3 DOF Type 1 about the vertex (3.4.1).
STEP 3  parallel or serial: 220 and 71 deg are beyond any parallel flexure; the system is SERIAL (Fig. 2.7, right branch).
STEP 4  intermediate freedom spaces from ground: [bearing: 1R vertical through F] -> [flexure: 1R horizontal through F] -> [flexure: 3 DOF Type 1 about the vertex].
        1 + 1 + 3 = 5 twists, the five wanted; the dish's roll about its own axis is fixed by no stage and needed by none: the chain is not underconstrained (p. 36).
STEP 5  ground = the deck and the tube (F is on the tube; the strip's own ring at the tube top turns with the fork). Stage 1 = the fork: a ring beam r 3.61 m
        on the deck rail and two posts up to the elevation axis. Stage 2 = the cradle: two side arms at |y| 2.45 m (outside the rim, 2.22 m), a back frame
        0.6 m behind the vertex, counterweights 2.6 m up-sun of the axis. Stage 3 = the dish on its back ring.

STEP 6  constraints from the constraint spaces

  trunnion block, stage B: rank 5, DOF 1: ['rotation about axis through [-0.  0. -0.] along [-0. -1. -0.]']
  reciprocal product of every blade line of both blocks with the rotation about the axis: 0.0e+00
  the two blocks together (one stage each, in parallel across the cradle): rank 5, DOF 1: ['rotation about axis through [0. 0. 0.] along [ 0. -1.  0.]']
        the same lines meet the axis whether the blocks sit at F or at |y| 3.25 m: the constraint space of a rotation does not depend on where along its axis
        the constraints are placed (thesis 3.2.1). At F they would cross the aperture in projection and the tube's reach; at |y| 3.25 m they do neither.
  blades 200 x 1.0 x 120 mm 17-7PH: +-25.8 deg per stage at 0.25 sigma_y; needed +-17.8 per stage (71 deg over two stages)
        -> 258 MPa = 0.17 sigma_y at el 12 and 83, neutral at el 47.5
  spring: 2EI/L = 55.6 N m/rad per crossed pair, 167 per stage, 83 per block (two stages in series), 167 N m/rad for the two blocks; 103 N m at the ends of travel
  cradle mass: dish 60 + frame 40 + arms 258 + water 2 x 131 + cranks 30 = 650 kg, balanced about the axis
  9 m/s: drag 751 N; its moment about F 3.0 kN m becomes +-0.46 kN along the dish axis at the two blocks 6.5 m apart;

        worst blade 1940 N in plane (25 m/s, crosswind, el 44) vs fixed-fixed buckling 9139 N -> SF 4.7
        the third pass put one block at F and counted forces only; the crosswind's 3.0 kN m at 9 m/s on cells 0.33 m apart would have loaded its blades 3.2 kN: over buckling. Two blocks fix that.

  azimuth: the fork's ring beam r 3.61 m on the deck rail (Hashemi's ring rail); its rollers' lines meet the vertical F-F2 axis, the 1R constraint space; 220 deg; a bearing, not a flexure.
  fine stage: three flat tangential blades at (60.0, 180.0, 300.0) deg from the slot (9 lines, all in the back plane) -> rank 3, DOF 3: ['rotation about axis through [ 0.  0. -0.] along [0. 1. 0.]', 'translation along [0. 0. 1.]', 'rotation about axis through [0. 0. 0.] along [1. 0. 0.]']

  blades 60 x 1.5 x 450 mm: +-20 mrad -> end offset 29 mm, S-bend 130 MPa = 0.09 sigma_y; tangential stiffness EA/L 40 MN/m;

ACTUATION  elevation: a crank 1.0 m from the axis on each arm (20 deg up-sun of the perpendicular) and a self-locking screw jack to a bracket 0.25 m behind and 2.75 m below the axis on each post:
        jack length 2.06-3.22 m (stroke 1.16), lever 0.81-1.00 m over el 12-83;
        9 m/s: wind torque about the axis 3.0 kN m (+ 103 N m of blade spring) -> 1.9 kN per jack at the worst lever
        25 m/s: wind torque about the axis 23.2 kN m (+ 103 N m of blade spring) -> 14.3 kN per jack at the worst lever
        holding stiffness ~50 MN m/rad per jack -> 30 urad under the 9 m/s mean; gusts inside the fine stage's +-20 mrad. No power to hold. Tr40 jacks, 50 kN, 1.2 m stroke.
        turgor: pumping 46 L from the up-sun tanks to a tank on the back frame cancels the 9 m/s mean torque; the jack holds the rest.
        azimuth: a pinion on the ring beam. fine: the columns on the beam-centroid loop at the tube's waist.

CLEARANCE  tube vs cradle and fork over 59 Quetta sun positions: worst 71 mm (dish back ring, summer 11.0 h, el 75)
        the arms at |y| 2.45 m clear the rim toroid (2.22 m) by 155 mm; the posts at |y| 3.61 m are outside the dish's sweep at every hour
        at el 12 the tank ends reach 1.6 m south of the tube axis at z 10.5; at el 83 they stand at z 12.8
        ring beam diameter 7.5 m inside the 7.8 m the dish's sweep already needs

SHADING  every mount part projects outside the aperture along the sun line by construction (arms and posts at |y| > 2.1 m, blocks beyond them,
        counterweights on the arms); shading.py rasterises the model to confirm it. The tube, the strip and its ring are the cass machine's own.

WHAT IS COMPLIANT HERE  the elevation pivots (two blocks of twelve blades, exact 1R about the axis through F, 71 deg, no bearing, no backlash), the fine stage
        (three blades, exact 3 DOF Type 1, microradians), the flexure struts of the actuators, and the posts as the thermal compliance of the trunnion pair.
        Not compliant and not pretending: the ring bearing, the tube, the arms, the jacks.
