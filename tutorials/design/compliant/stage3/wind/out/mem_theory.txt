4. TENSION DRIFT the control has to chase: f = a^2/(4 s0) with s0 = p a^2/(4 T) -> f follows T/p one to one
   at  1.0 m/s the film runs 4.7 K above the air: thermal strain 0.008 % against a working strain of 2.66 % -> tension 0.30 % lower, f 1.2 cm shorter (the 7 pressure levels span +-20 %): negligible
   at  3.0 m/s the film runs 2.6 K above the air: thermal strain 0.004 % against a working strain of 2.66 % -> tension 0.17 % lower, f 0.7 cm shorter (the 7 pressure levels span +-20 %): negligible
   at 12.0 m/s the film runs 0.9 K above the air: thermal strain 0.001 % against a working strain of 2.66 % -> tension 0.06 % lower, f 0.2 cm shorter (the 7 pressure levels span +-20 %): negligible
   PET creep at 60 MPa: ~1 % per decade of hours -> ~38 % of the working strain per decade: a slow pressure trim, seasonal not gusty
