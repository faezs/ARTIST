First pass · catalogue elements, superseded

Mirror 1 · primary pumped-membrane dish, a = 2.10 m

Pitch, figure and carriage on Hashemi's fixed-focus mount

Kept for the numbers in their title blocks; the topologies above replace them. The dish orbits the focus at one focal length with its attitude slaved to a focus-centred arc. Sliding the fold's shadow off the dish needs a β/2 pitch the carriage does not have; correcting the sphere's off-axis astigmatism needs the rim to become a weak toroid. Both are flexures here. The rollers and tow-wire keep rolling parts only where a 6.6 m travel forces them.

First pass · superseded

Mirror 2 · beam-down fold at the focus F

The mirror hangs face-down at F inside a fixed hood; its normal bisects the incoming beam and the vertical, so the tilt is 45° − el_b/2 and the azimuth comes free from the carriage beam. The flexures sit on the back and never see the beam. Gravity preloads the chain, and the unpowered state is the parked, beam-dumped one.

First pass · superseded

Mirror 3 · M5 ellipsoidal relay, 43 facets

Each facet stands on three folded bipod feet riding parallel-motion carriages: an exact constraint that is athermal by symmetry, adjusted by hard-contact detent screws so no held position depends on a stressed flexure. The design finding that changed the build: the facets must be pressed toroids, not rolled strips.

Source of every number: the three memos on branch compliant-mechanisms under tutorials/design/compliant/ (primary_dish.md, beam_down_fold.md, m5_relay.md), designed against Howell, Magleby & Olsen, Handbook of Compliant Mechanisms (Wiley 2013).
