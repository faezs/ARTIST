Fifth pass · the flower proper · one stem, the membrane on the crown

One stem, a crown of branches, the exact spherical membrane on their tips, F on the light pipe

This is the flower as it was described from the start, read literally at last: one trunk, a 1 m stem; lots of branches rooted on it, whose tips form the scatter pattern on which the membrane rests; the foliage shaped in one direction because the head always stands on the anti-sun side of F; and the surface cut out of that foliage a spherical section, not a paraboloid, because the primary of hashemi.ini is the sphere of radius 8 m that has no optical axis. That is what makes one stem enough: a paraboloid on the orbit needs its vertex at F - 4 s and its axis along s, and a stem from one root cannot give both (the second pass); the sphere needs only its centre of curvature at F + 4 s, and its attitude about that centre is the freedom the env already spends as beta_dev. The light pipe is not part of the tree: it is the cass machine's bore on the roof, the hyperboloid strip at its top before F, M4 and the duct below, a Masdar beam-down in miniature. The sheets put the head where the env puts it (beta_dev 0, retro) at five suns; the sweep sheet shows what the crown must do to follow it, and the wind row what it must hold: 0.75 kN at 9 m/s to 5 cm at F. The audit of the earlier passes' calculations and the wind gating of every machine are in progress in a separate pass and will replace the numbers they touch.
