**Gaps:**
- No mass, inertia, stiffness, drag coefficient or structural model exists anywhere in the env; every mechanical load above is a hand estimate on top of the code's geometry and pressure law (q = 0.6 v^2, membrane FvK).
- The Hashemi env inherits the polar wind path (wind x 0.4 courtyard shelter, stow at 16/14 m/s) rather than the base env's wind_limit 9/7; its modelled wind never exceeds ~5.6 m/s and it never stows, so the code provides no wind load spectrum for a rooftop dish. The brief's 9 m/s working case and 25 m/s survival case are therefore design choices, not code-derived; a real 50-yr design gust at lat 28.6 is ~47 m/s.
- The OU gust model (sigma 1.8 m/s, tau 600 s) has no energy above ~10 mHz: fatigue micro-cycle counts for the post, the tow wire, the pitch flexure and the rollers need a real turbulence spectrum (Kaimal/von Karman) that the code does not contain.
- The receiver-at-focus fold (0.4 m mirror at F, 9-14 m post) does not exist in this worktree: the env's fold is a 1.17 m flat 4.77 m above the waist on a 5.8 m skeletal mast above the deck. Post height, section and head mass are assumed.
- Pretension mismatch: the env runs T_pre = 600 N/m (polar override) while the primary-dish memo sized the rim, squeeze and hoop loads at 2000 N/m. The plenum pressures and film tensions reported here use 600; the design must pick one.
- Not in code and estimated here: film areal density and rim tube size, jam-bed mass, carriage sled mass, roller spacing, tow-wire pretension and rope grade, coating absorptances (especially any broadband 'dielectric' mirror), IFB/hood absorptance, pit air temperature and its diurnal swing, in-pit wind velocity fraction, M5 frame mass per m2 at 3 m radius, facet-foot base geometry.
- Level-change frequency of the plenum (the policy can change level every 15 s step) and the fraction of days with the winter beta seam were estimated, not measured from rollouts.
- The tandoor pit frame constants (R_POT 0.42, Z_DUCT -0.86 mouth frame, H_DEPTH 2.44, R_SPH 2.54 m belly, coude Z_DUCT 0.14 / R_DUCT_C 0.20) were read but do not enter any mirror load case; the pit sphere and node areas are thermal, not structural, inputs.
